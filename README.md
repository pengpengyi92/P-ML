# P-ML

An ongoing public workspace for machine learning research, reproducible experiments, and learning from first principles. Formerly **ML-Architectures**.

## From ML-Architectures to P-ML: An Open Machine Learning Research Statement

**Published:** `2026-11-14T03:16:18+08:00` (Asia/Shanghai, UTC+08:00)

ML-Architectures began in April 2025 as a small teaching and learning project about what happens inside machine learning models. P-ML keeps those foundations and expands them into an ongoing public home for learning, original ML research, reproductions, and curated resources. We will publish what we try, what we measure, what fails, and what remains open to question.

P University is a **private** research initiative. P-ML may cite or adapt selected material from its Department of CSDSAI when it has been cleared for public release. This does not open the private workspace or its underlying materials. P-ML also conducts research directly in public, independently of that source.

Bring your own algorithms, research questions, replication attempts, negative results, or useful ML resources. Use Discussions to explore ideas, Issues to propose submissions or track concrete work, and pull requests to add entries or reproducible research.

## Explore the repository

**Priority expansion · [CV / DL / RL:43 new primary repositories](ml-repo/cv-dl-rl/README.md)**
adds16 computer-vision,11 deep-learning and16 reinforcement-learning projects. The
combined canonical catalog now has116 unique projects. Deep learning is a continuing
focus: gradients, training state, fine-tuning, parallelism and reliable evaluation.
[Focus report](report/2026-11-15-cv-dl-rl.md). Source/rights checks are not model execution.

**2026-11-15 · [ML ecosystem map:73 primary repositories /15 routes](ml-repo/ecosystem/README.md)**
covers frameworks, supervised/unsupervised/self-supervised learning, RL, vision, audio,
probabilistic/time-series/graph learning, evaluation and serving. Source pins, rights,
archives and task-specific experiment protocols accompany the catalog.
[Investigation report](report/2026-11-15-ml-ecosystem.md). This is a bounded curated map,
not all ML software or a claim to have run every library. Prior10 detailed cards remain.

Start with the [10-repository ML reading guide](ml-repo/README.md): classical
models, deep learning, LLM tools, data and evaluation. Each card states why it
belongs in P-ML, pinned source entry points, learning questions and unrun exercises.
[repo/](repo/README.md) is a navigation alias; ml-repo/ remains canonical.

| Area | What belongs here |

## Contribute

- [Share your ML / DL / RL work (#2)](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/P-ML/issues/2): submit a repo, public paper, research idea, replication or negative result. Choose **ML / DL / RL / Combined**.
- [Research Exchange (#3)](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/P-ML/discussions/3): discuss the work and find the credited, curated community index. One original thread per work; cross-link instead of reposting.
- [General call for contributions (#1)](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/P-ML/issues/1) stays open for broader suggestions. Use [Issues](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/P-ML/issues) for corrections or concrete experiment proposals.
- Pull requests: follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Both maintainer-curated and community submissions are welcome.

P-ML welcomes AI-native collaboration: agents can help document, verify and
draft contributions with disclosure, human review and authorization before posting.
